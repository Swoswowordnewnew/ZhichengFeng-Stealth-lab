PHYSICAL BASIS

Linking local evidence to far-field effects

These equations explain the method. Calibration operators, probe parameters, inversion weights, and engineering thresholds are outside the public demo.

01Local impedance and reflection

Γᵖ = (Zᵢₙᵖ − Z₀ᵖ) / (Zᵢₙᵖ + Z₀ᵖ)

Repair materials, adhesive, disbonds, and core damage change local input impedance and reflection amplitude and phase.

02Differential near fields

ΔENF = Erepair − Ereference

Compare with an intact reference to isolate new perturbations. A local response alone cannot explain total aircraft scattering.

03Equivalent surface currents

ΔJs = n̂ × ΔH　·　ΔMs = −n̂ × ΔE

Express differential near fields as equivalent sources to connect local changes with far-field effects.

04Coherent far-field superposition

E∞repair ≈ E∞intact + ΔE∞local

Retain complex phase to account for constructive and destructive interference with other scattering centers.
